Established in 2026, Bohlale Hoodies is a new player in the South African streetwear scene,specialising exclusively in hoodies.

The founders identified a gap in the local market for high quality hoodies that tell a story.

Rather than mass production, Bohlale Hoodies adopts a “drop” model,releasing small limited batches of hoodies to create excitement and exclusivity.

The brand is built on the principles of quality craftsmanship,community and cultural pride.
